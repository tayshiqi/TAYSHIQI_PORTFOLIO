Stone Fruits

Momo

Region: Mainly grown in Fukushima, Yamanashi, and Okayama Prefectures.
Season: Late summer (July to August).
Nutritional Benefits: Rich in vitamins A and C, high in water content, and contains antioxidants that help protect against cellular damage and aging.

Ume

Region: Grown predominantly in Wakayama and Yamagata Prefectures.
Season: Late spring to early summer (May to June).
Nutritional Benefits: Rich in antioxidants, helping reduce inflammation and oxidative stress.

Sakuranbo

Region: Grown in Yamagata, Aomori, and Nagano Prefectures.
Season: Late spring to early summer (May to June).
Nutritional Benefits: High in antioxidants, including anthocyanins, which help protect heart health.

  • Shop Now

Anzu

Region: Grown mainly in Yamagata and Nagano Prefectures.
Season: Early summer (June to July).
Nutritional Benefits: Rich in dietary fiber, aiding digestion and maintaining healthy blood sugar levels.

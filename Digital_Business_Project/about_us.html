About Us

Kanso Fruit

Company Background

Kanso Fruits is founded in 2024, a Malaysian e-commerce company specializing in importing premium Japanese fruits. Guided by the slogan "Authentic Flavors, Naturally Sweet," we deliver high-quality, fresh fruits sourced from Japan's renowned agricultural farms. By connecting Japanese farmers to global markets, we aim to expand access to Japan's finest produce. As importers of nature’s finest, we bring the best seasonal harvests to discerning customers who value quality, freshness, and authenticity.

Mission

  • Offer unique fruit experiences through top-quality Japanese fruits from Japan’s best orchards.
  • Showcase Japanese farmers’ expertise with premium, seasonal, citrus, and stone fruits.
  • Ensure customer satisfaction with efficient online shopping and delivery services.

Vision

  • Become a leading online platform for Japanese fruits, emphasizing quality and specialty.
  • Foster understanding of Japanese agriculture and its commitment to high-quality food.
  • Promote a healthier lifestyle and culinary creativity through educational talks and expertise-sharing events.

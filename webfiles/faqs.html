Frequently Asked Questions

What are Brewtopia’s opening hours? +

We’re open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM, and Saturday to Sunday from 9:00 AM – 11:00 PM.

Does Brewtopia offer vegetarian or vegan options? +

Yes! We have a selection of vegetarian and vegan pastries, sandwiches, and plant-based milk alternatives for your drinks.

Can I order online or for takeaway? +

Absolutely. You can order directly at the café for takeaway, or place your order online through our website and delivery partners.

Do you provide free Wi-Fi and charging stations? +

Yes, Brewtopia offers free Wi-Fi and charging outlets, making it the perfect spot to study, work, or meet with friends.

Where do you source your coffee beans from? +

We use ethically sourced beans from trusted local roasters and international suppliers to ensure freshness, quality, and sustainability.

Can I book Brewtopia for private events? +

Yes! Our café space is available for small gatherings, workshops, and private events. Contact us for reservations and details.

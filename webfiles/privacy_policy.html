Privacy Policy

At Brewtopia Café, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you interact with our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Your name and email address (e.g., when you subscribe to our newsletter or send us a message).
  • Contact details such as phone number (if you make a reservation or inquiry).
  • Basic browsing information through cookies (to improve your website experience).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to your inquiries or feedback.
  • Send you updates, promotions, and special offers (only if you subscribe).
  • Improve our website, products, and services.

3. How We Protect Your Information

We take appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against loss, misuse, or unauthorized access. Your data will not be shared with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

5. Your Rights

  • Request corrections to your personal data.
  • Unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time.
